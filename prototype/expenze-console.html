Policy rules

Per-head meal cap

Declared natively per currency. A receipt in a currency with no cap goes to review rather than through an invented exchange rate.

Never reimbursable

Excluded outright, whatever the amount or headcount. Removed from the bill before the cap is applied.

Tax & service charge on excluded items

A bar bill with a separate tax line currently reimburses the tax on the alcohol. This is the fix.

Channels & API

WhatsApp

connector not built

Staff photograph a receipt and send it to this number. Matched to a person by the mobile number they send from, so anyone without a mobile on file is unmatched.

Email intake

connector not built

Forward an invoice or booking confirmation. Matched by the sending address. Attachments and inline images are both read.

REST API

POST https://api.expenze.ai/v1/receipts endpoint live (API Gateway)

Third-party systems submit on a person's behalf. Identify them by staff_id, email or mobile.

Example call

curl -X POST https://api.expenze.ai/v1/receipts \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $EXPENZE_API_KEY" \
  -d '{ "staff_id": "M80-4417",
        "idempotency_key": "trip-8821-dinner",
        "image_url": "https://.../receipt.jpg" }'

A repeated idempotency_key returns the original result and never spends a second credit.

Real: the policy engine is a port of lambda_src/policy.py, so every verdict, deduction, queue count and auto-clear rate recomputes live from the rules above. Staged: extraction (line items are pre-filled, not read from the model), the WhatsApp and email connectors, credit purchase, and sign-in — identity here is a prototype switcher, not real auth. Only the REST endpoint is actually deployed. In production the three roles live in your IdP, and every decision writes the reviewer's ID to DynamoDB alongside the verdict.
